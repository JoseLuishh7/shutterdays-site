Shutterdays Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026
Contact: Shutterdays, hernandezj@shutterdaysdev.tech

Shutterdays is a photo journal: one photo a day, seven days a week. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Things you give us

  • Your email address, used to sign you in with a one-time code. We don't use passwords.
  • Your profile: name (username), optional bio, optional profile photo, the cameras you list, and your time zone. Your time zone decides which day each photo counts for.
  • Your daily photos and anything you add to them: the camera you pick and an optional field note (up to 140 characters).
  • Your activity: who you follow and who follows you, follow requests, awards you give, accounts you block, and photos you report.

Things collected from your photos

  • Camera details stored with each photo: camera model, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed, ISO and the time the photo was taken. They appear when someone taps your photo.
  • We never keep location. Daily photos and profile photos are resized and re-saved on your phone before upload. This removes GPS data and the rest of the original file's metadata; only the image size and orientation remain.

Things collected automatically

  • A push notification token for your device, so we can tell you about new followers, requests, awards and when your Darkroom opens. You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings.
  • Crash reports are on in every installed version of the app. They contain technical details such as device model, operating system and what the app was doing, to help us fix bugs. They don't include your photos.
  • Connection logs. Our servers and service providers log technical details such as your IP address and device type when the app connects to them.
  • Daily reminder settings are stored only on your phone.

We don't use advertising, ad trackers or analytics that follow you across other apps.

Who can see what

  • Public account (the default): anyone signed in to Shutterdays can see your profile, photos, field notes, camera details and reels.
  • Private account: your name, profile photo, bio and counts are visible to signed-in people so they can ask to follow you. Your photos and reels are only visible to followers you approve.
  • Award counts on a photo are only shown to you, the photo's owner.
  • Blocking someone hides your content from each other and removes any follows between you.
  • Hidden photos are only visible to you.

How we use your information

  • To run the app: sign you in, show your week, build your feed and your weekly reels, and send the notifications you allow.
  • To keep the community safe: review reports, remove content that breaks the rules, and suspend accounts.
  • To fix problems and improve the app, using crash reports.

We don't sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We use a few service providers to run Shutterdays. They process data on our behalf and only as needed:

  • Supabase stores your account, profile, photos and activity. Region: United States (Oregon).
  • Expo builds and distributes test versions of the app and delivers push notifications.
  • Resend sends your sign-in codes.
  • Sentry receives crash reports.
  • Apple and Google distribute the app. Their own privacy policies apply to the App Store and Google Play.

These providers are required to protect your information in line with this policy and may not use it for their own purposes.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users.

How long we keep it

  • We keep your information while your account exists.
  • Deleting your account (Settings → Delete account) permanently removes your profile, photos, reels, field notes, follows, awards and notifications, and deletes your photo files from storage. Deletion is immediate: we don't keep backup copies of your data.
  • Reports you made about other people's photos are deleted with your account.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile, switch to a private account, hide your photos, or block people at any time.
  • Turn push notifications and the daily reminder on or off.
  • Delete your account in Settings at any time.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your data. Contact us at hernandezj@shutterdaysdev.tech and we'll respond within 30 days.

Children

Shutterdays is not meant for anyone under 15. We don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
